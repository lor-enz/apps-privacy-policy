**Effective date:** DATE

This privacy policy applies to the Android app **APP NAME**, published by **Lorenz Burghardt** ("I", "me") on Google Play.

## Data the app uses

- **WHAT.** Why the app uses it, and that it stays on your device.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's saved settings in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account and its settings. I have no access to it.

I do not sell, share or transfer any data, because the app does not collect any.

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

To delete the app's saved settings, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.
