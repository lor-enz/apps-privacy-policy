# Privacy policy for Can I go home yet

**Effective date:** 30 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android app **Can I go home yet**, published by **Lorenz Burghardt** ("I", "me") on Google Play.

## Summary

Can I go home yet does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

## Data the app uses

- **Start time and break length.** You enter these to calculate when your work day ends. They are used only on your device and are not sent anywhere.
- **Daily work time.** The work duration you set is saved on your device so you don't have to enter it again.

The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. It does not request any Android permissions, including internet access, so it cannot send data off your device.

## Device backup

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the saved daily work time in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account and its settings. I have no access to it.

## Sharing

I do not sell, share or transfer any data, because the app does not collect any.

## Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

To delete the saved daily work time, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
